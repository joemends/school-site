Our School

Welcome to ViviChild Academy

ViviChild Academy is a private educational institution in Gbawe, Weija-Gbawe Municipal, providing children with quality education built on strong moral foundations. We combine structured academics with developmental activities, ICT exposure and a genuinely supportive environment, so every child feels safe, seen and encouraged to grow.

From the earliest years through basic education, our teachers focus on the whole child — mind, character and confidence — preparing learners for the next stage of their journey and for life beyond the classroom.

Our Story

How ViviChild Academy Began

ViviChild Academy was founded on a simple belief: that every child in Gbawe deserves an education that nurtures their whole self — mind, character and confidence — not just their grades. What began as a small, closely-knit classroom has grown into a full learning community serving Early Childhood, Nursery, Kindergarten and Basic Education, built one relationship at a time with the parents and children of Gbawe and the wider Weija-Gbawe Municipal area.

Our mission is to provide quality, child-centred education inside a safe, disciplined and caring environment — one where teachers know each child by name, where moral formation matters as much as academic milestones, and where ICT and modern learning tools are introduced thoughtfully rather than for their own sake.

We measure our progress not only in test scores but in the confidence, curiosity and character our learners carry with them long after they leave our classrooms — and we're just getting started.
