Our School

Welcome to ViviChild Academy

ViviChild Academy is a private educational institution in Gbawe, Weija-Gbawe Municipal, providing children with quality education built on strong moral foundations. We combine structured academics with developmental activities, ICT exposure and a genuinely supportive environment, so every child feels safe, seen and encouraged to grow.

From the earliest years through basic education, our teachers focus on the whole child — mind, character and confidence — preparing learners for the next stage of their journey and for life beyond the classroom.
